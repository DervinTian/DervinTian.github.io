MDP: Photorealistic Street Maps

1/2026 - Present

The University of Michigan's Multidisciplinary Design Program, also known as MDP, is a program that aims to provide studnets with research and design experience by parterning with real-world companies. My project, sponsored by Qualcomm aims to make an open-source alternative to Google Earth where the user can specify a location in the world and receive a 3D model of the selected area.

The project began on January 7 and since then, our team has set up a consistent meeting schedule with our sponsors representatives from Qualcomm to provide updates, ensuring an Agile workspace and ensuring that we are staying on track. Outside of meeting with sponsors, we also hold our own personal student meetings where we go over project tasks, program logistics, and build up our connection as a team.

Project Overview: https://mdp.engin.umich.edu/sponsor_teams/qualcomm-26

MAAV: Software Lead

9/2025 - Present

Michigan Autonomous Aerial Vehicles, also known as MAAV, is a student project team here at the Univeristy of Michigan. Our mission is to create competitive autonomous drones capable of autonomous flight, computer vision and computer networking. I am currently the software lead and working on designing the software for a fleet of drones capable of competing in the IARC mission 10 competition.

To streamline the recruitment process and provide a seamless experience for prospective members, I set up a auto emailing service using Google Apps Script to provide new members with the necessary information to get started and introduce them to each subteam, helping them identify their best fit. Our team strives to build a fun and inclusive environment for all members, regardless of status by organizing team dinners, team game nights, as well organizing field trips. We hope that our team environment allows members to build a sense of camaraderie and collaboration that helps us work better not only as an engineering team, but also get along as a massive group of friends.

Learn more about us: https://maavumich.org

Toyoda Gosei North America: Data Science Intern

5/2025 - 8/2025

Toyoda Gosei is a global manufacturere of automotive components with headquarters across Japan, USA, and more. Between May 2025 to August 2025, I worked as a data science intern. One of my first projects was streamlining data workflows in Microsoft Fabric, setting up data warehouses in order to transform, analyze, and pipeline data, creating a unified hub for efficient data processing. I also worked with machine learning models during my internship, namely using a Recurrent Neural Network in order to forecast future data readings by analzying historical trends. At the end of the internship program, I presented my progress and projects in a slideshow to my supervisors and fellow interns.

During my internship, I also got to visit many other Toyoda Gosei facilities, such as their technical center to learn about how they conduct their many rigourous safety tests, and also got to visit their manufacturing plant to see in-person how parts are mass produced.
