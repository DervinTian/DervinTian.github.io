Michigan Skate Collective

1/2024 - Present

University of Michigan's Skate Collective is the culmination of wheels. We welcome skateboarders, roller skaters, inline skaters, unicyclists, and everything in between! I am a skateboarder that regularly attends skate sessions with a mission of spreading the joy of skateboarding to anyone and everyone who is willing to accept it. I've been on trips to other massive skateparks across Michigan and have made some of my best friends during my time in college.

Spray Paint Art

12/2023 - Present

I've been creating spray paint art for almost 2 years now, after first learning about it from the streets of New York City. Seeing how intricate many of those paintings can get, through the creative use of plastic tupperware, spray paint cans and paper stensils, I decided that it was something that I wanted to try. With the only limitation being my imagination, as well as my supply of rubber latex gloves, I've had a blast creating my own custom posters and you can find them linked above.

Rubik's Cubes

2015 - Present

I first got into cubing when I was in elementary school as it was something new and fun. Not only did I get to be the cool kid in class for a week, I also learned about the immpact that consistent practice can make. Throughout the span of a couple of years, I was able to lower my personal best from a time of around a minute, down to a mere 9.20 seconds, an achievement that I am extremely proud of to this day. While it can be considered a niche hobby, I have found a suprisingly large community of like-minded Cubers that I've been able to engage with and have a blast with.
